01 / The why

I wanted more than an AI that remembers my name.

Most of us use AI to write, research, plan and solve problems. But useful context often has to be re-explained: how we think, what we value, how we work and what good output looks like.

What if I could build an AI environment that understands my operating principles and helps me think, learn and execute with greater continuity?

Not an AI that replaces me. Not one that pretends to be me. A system designed to work alongside me.

02 / The idea

A cognitive twin is not just a biography in a chatbot.

IDENTITY

What I have done, what I know, the projects I work on and the context that matters.

COGNITIVE FRAMEWORK

How I approach problems, evaluate evidence, make decisions and communicate.

EXECUTION

How the assistant helps turn ideas into research, decisions, working systems and deliverables.

The goal is cognitive augmentation: extend my agency, not replace it.

03 / Four operating modes

Different modes. One coherent working relationship.

Select a mode to explore how the Digital Double is designed to support a task.

MODE A

Structural Mirror & Editor

“I have a collection of rough thoughts. Help me organise them without losing my voice.”

Designed response: structure the material, preserve the original meaning and flag any substantive interpretation rather than silently changing the position.

04 / Operating principles

How I want the system to work.

CONTEXT BEFORE OUTPUT

Understand the problem, constraints and intended outcome before producing an answer.

CLARITY OVER COMPLEXITY

Make difficult ideas understandable without stripping away what makes them useful.

VOICE & AGENCY

Preserve my voice; don't force generic formulas or make decisions on my behalf.

APPLIED VERIFICATION

If we claim to know something, we should be able to verify, test or demonstrate it.

05 / What comes next

Version 1.0 is a foundation, not the finish line.

The next phase is to consolidate my ChatGPT conversation export and a Google Takeout archive spanning 67 product exports. The intention is to classify, validate and organise information—not to turn every historical detail into permanent memory.

v1.0 · Operating identity→Knowledge consolidation→Evidence-backed context→Portable AI infrastructure

Future integrations and automation are possibilities to build and validate, not capabilities claimed as complete.

06 / Your turn

What would you want your own cognitive twin to help you do?

Understand how you think? Challenge your ideas? Organise your knowledge? Help execute your projects?

I'm exploring what becomes possible when we move beyond one-off prompts and start building personal AI systems around the way we think and work.
